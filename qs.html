Tie Wire Estimation

Fajardo's methods for #16 G.I. Tie Wire:

  • For CHB: Multiply total length of CHB rebars by 0.016 kg/m.
  • For Heavy Framing: Use 1.0 kg per 100 kg of rebar.
  • For Slabs/Tight Grids: Use 1.5 kg per 100 kg of rebar.

Direct Method for Structural Rebars

Fajardo emphasizes determining the Exact Pieces of Commercial Bars (6m, 7.5m, 9m, 10.5m, 12m) over pure volume. Always calculate:

  1. Cut Length (subtract concrete cover, usually 40mm or 75mm).
  2. Number of Cuts (Span ÷ Spacing + 1).
  3. Pieces per Bar (Commercial Length ÷ Cut Length).
  4. Total Bars needed (Total Cuts ÷ Pieces per Bar).

Chapter 4: Lumber

Board Foot Formula

Used for all wooden structural members. T = thickness (inches), W = width (inches), L = length (feet).

BF = (T × W × L) / 12

*Remember: Never mix units. T & W must be in inches. L must be in feet.

Chapter 5: Scaffolds, Forms & Staging

Plywood Formworks

A standard 4' x 8' (1.2m x 2.4m) plywood has an effective area of:

2.88 sq.m

Formula: Total Surface Area to be Formed ÷ 2.88

  • Columns: Perimeter × Height
  • Beams: (2 × Depth + Width) × Length
  • Slabs: Floor Area (bottom only)

Formworks Lumber & Nails

Fajardo's fast method for Formworks support systems:

1. Framing Lumber (Studs/Walers):

Multiply the total Plywood Area (in sq.m) by 24.0 to 29.0 to get the approximate total Board Feet of lumber.

2. Assorted Nails:

  • Estimate 1.0 to 1.5 kg of nails for every 100 Board Feet of framing lumber.
  • Use 2" nails for plywood to studs.
  • Use 3" to 4" nails for 2x2 or 2x3 lumber connections.
